E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Analysis

How much does the store earn, where does the revenue come from, and which customers wait too long for their orders? Real data from Olist, a Brazilian online marketplace, 2016–2018.

What I did
  • Created 5 tables in PostgreSQL and imported 99K+ orders and 112K+ order items.
  • Wrote SQL with JOINs, GROUP BY, CASE WHEN and date functions to analyse revenue, categories, states and delivery times.
  • Built a SQL view joining all tables, with calculated delivery status and delivery days.
  • Connected Power BI to PostgreSQL and checked the dashboard totals matched the SQL results exactly.
Findings
R$13.2Mrevenue from 96K delivered orders, R$137 per order
38%of revenue comes from São Paulo alone
8.1%of orders arrive late, about 1 in 12
25+ dayswait in northern states, versus under 10 in São Paulo

RecommendationAdd delivery capacity for the north and northeast. Late deliveries spiked in Nov 2017 and peaked at about 21% in Mar 2018, so plan extra capacity for busy periods.

ExcelPower BIDAX9,994 orders

Sales Performance & Profit Analysis

The business sells well, so where is it losing money, and why? Sample Superstore retail data, 2014–2017.

What I did
  • Checked the data in Excel: blanks, duplicate rows and a pivot table to validate totals.
  • Built a date table and DAX measures for sales, profit, profit margin, orders and total loss.
  • Added discount bands to measure how discounts affect profit.
  • Used green and red conditional formatting so losses stand out at a glance.
Findings
12.5%profit margin on $2.3M sales and $286K profit
>20%discount turns orders from profit into loss
-77%margin on orders discounted above 40%
$18Kprofit from Furniture on $742K of sales

RecommendationCap discounts at 20% and review pricing for Tables and Bookcases, the biggest loss-makers.

HR Employee Attrition Analysis

Why do employees leave, and which groups are most at risk? IBM HR Analytics dataset.

What I did
  • Grouped employees by age, tenure, salary and satisfaction with DAX calculated columns.
  • Compared attrition rates instead of raw counts, so groups of different sizes compare fairly.
  • Wrote each chart title as its finding, so the story reads in seconds.
Findings
16.1%overall attrition, 237 of 1,470 employees
~40%of Sales Representatives leave, over double the average
36%attrition among employees aged 18–25
35%attrition in the first year at the company

RecommendationFocus retention on young, new and lower-paid staff, especially in Sales, through onboarding and pay reviews.
